Community Planning

Vista Design has provided professional land planning services for some of the most prestigious and financially successful subdivisions, planned communities, multifamily developments and mixed-use developments found in our market area. We endeavor to optimize land use and promote sustainable development patterns by utilizing the land so as to meet present human needs, while sufficiently preserving the environment to fulfill future needs and economic interests. Furthermore, we advocate the principles of "smart growth," whereby development is centralized to the fullest extent possible, thus reducing sprawl, maximizing green space, creating a sense of community and minimizing infrastructure costs. The scope of our community planning services and experience is multi-faceted, encompassing concept development, land planning, infrastructure engineering, landscape / hardscape design, amenities design, and surveying, thereby enabling your project to be completed at the lowest possible cost.
