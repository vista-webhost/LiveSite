Vista Design: The premier Land Planning and Landscape Architecture firm on Delmarva

Vista Design, Inc. is one of the Mid-Atlantic region's foremost Civil Engineering, Surveying, Land Planning, Landscape Architecture and Architecture Services firms. The central location of our office on Maryland's Eastern Shore enables us to provide expedient and cost-effective professional services to clients throughout Maryland, Delaware and Virginia, including the major markets of Baltimore / Washington, Hampton Roads, and Wilmington / New Castle.

The land speaks to those who are able to interpret its message. Vista Design is listening. By capturing and defining the natural essence of the land, then harmoniously blending its unique qualities into the vision of our client we create exceptional designs. Excellence is achieved by more than just technical competence alone. There is a certain quiescent artistry also essential to the innovation of truly distinctive, cutting-edge designs. Vista's genius is founded in our ability to thoughtfully combine the arts and sciences of land planning, engineering, architecture, surveying and landscape design to create beauty, balance and function. Vista Design's mastery of these crafts, in addition to an unyielding commitment to superior customer service has been fundamental to our success and growth over the years, thus setting us apart from the competition.

Vista Design, Inc. offers a diverse range of professional services that includes, but is not limited to:

  • Civil Engineering
  • Surveying & Construction Stakeout
  • Community, Site & Commercial Land Planning
  • Architecture Services
  • Amenities, Trails, Parks & Open Space Design
  • Landscape / Hardscape Design
  • MDE-Compliant Environmental Site Design (ESD)
  • NPDES-Compliant Stormwater Management Design
  • MS4 Permitting Assistance
  • Critical Area Buffer Zone Design
  • Wetland Permitting & Mitigation
  • Stream / Habitat Restoration Design
  • Nutrient Management
  • Construction Progress Monitoring & Administration
